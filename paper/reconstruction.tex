\section{The Reconstruction Gap: A Controlled Negative Result}\label{sec:gap}

Sections~\ref{sec:recover}--\ref{sec:reconstruct} established that every datum Apple ships for
\code{afmplus-v11.x-ifp} can be recovered: all $218$ weight tensors, the $2$-bit codebook, the
per-output-row scales, the per-layer normalisation stream, the tokenizer, and the embedding
matrix. This section reports what happens when those recovered components are assembled into a
forward pass, and it is a negative result: \emph{the reconstruction does not predict text.}

We report it in full because the negative is unusually well constrained. Over $42$ controlled
experiments we eliminate every hypothesis class constructible from the shipped artifacts, and in
the process we characterise three evaluation instruments whose failure modes are, we believe, of
independent interest to anyone attempting from-weights reconstruction of a quantised model.

\subsection{The reconstruction gap, quantified}

Let $\ell(\mathcal{M})$ denote mean log-rank of the true next token under model $\mathcal{M}$ on
held-out natural text,
\[
  \ell(\mathcal{M}) \;=\; \frac{1}{T-1}\sum_{t=1}^{T-1}
  \log\bigl(1 + \bigl|\{v : z_v^{(t)} > z_{x_{t+1}}^{(t)}\}\bigr|\bigr),
\]
with $z^{(t)}$ the logits at position $t$. Define the \emph{$0$-layer floor} $\ell_0$ as the same
quantity computed on the embedding read out directly through the tied unembedding, with all $56$
transformer blocks bypassed. The floor is the un-gameable reference: it is what the embedding
alone knows, and any working reconstruction must beat it by a wide margin.

\midrule
Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct & 24 & 9.4617 & 1.7718 & $-7.6900$ \\
Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct & 28 & 9.2452 & 1.4673 & $-7.7779$ \\

\bottomrule

\begin{finding}
A working transformer converts depth into roughly $-7.7$ nats of predictive value. The
reconstruction converts depth into $+0.47$ nats: its layers do not merely fail to help, they
destroy information the embedding already carries.
\end{finding}

The per-depth curve shows this is not a late-layer collapse. Scoring the residual at every depth,
the reconstruction is at its floor through depth $\approx 8$, and degrades monotonically
thereafter, peaking at $+0.73$ near depth $48$. Its best truncation is depth $2$ at $-0.018$, which
is within noise. By contrast both controls are below their floors by mid-stack. Notably the
reconstruction's floor is itself \emph{good}: relative to the uniform baseline $\log(V/2)$ it sits
$4.66$ nats below chance where Qwen2.5-0.5B sits $1.78$ below, so the embedding decode is sound and
it is the layers that are inert.

\subsection{Instrument calibration, and three degeneracies}

The central methodological lesson of this work is that the obvious evaluation statistics are each
maximised by a different pathology, and that an uncalibrated instrument produces confident false
negatives. We document three.

\paragraph{Log-rank is gameable by inaction.} A reconstruction that writes nothing scores exactly
$\ell_0$, which is better than any partially-broken forward pass. Ranking candidate conventions by
log-rank therefore selects for \emph{silence}. In our case it selected a per-layer gamma
convention that attenuated the dense feed-forward branch by a factor of $229$, and it endorsed
branch-amplification sweeps that could only ever discover the do-nothing direction.

\paragraph{Causal signal growth is gameable by noise.} To escape the inaction degeneracy we
adopted a causal statistic: perturb the token at position $j$ and measure the growth of
$\lVert\Delta h_j\rVert$ from layer $0$ to the final layer. Healthy models amplify the perturbation
by $\times 59$ (Qwen-0.5B) and $\times 83$ (Qwen-1.5B); the reconstruction manages $\times 1.5$. An
inert model scores $\times 1$, so this statistic cannot be won by doing less. It can, however, be
won by \emph{doing more}: a sweep of $64$ normalisation-convention arms produced a candidate
scoring $\times 7.76$, a $5.1\times$ improvement, which on re-scoring against real-text log-rank was
\emph{worse} than the baseline ($9.27$ versus $7.59$). Amplifying every write propagates
perturbations beautifully while destroying prediction.

\begin{finding}
Log-rank and causal signal growth fail in mirror-image ways. No candidate may be accepted on
either alone; a valid claim requires log-rank below the $0$-layer floor \emph{and} healthy causal
dynamics.
\end{finding}

\paragraph{A truncated model can be blind to the component under test.} Most of our sweeps were
initially run on a $12$-layer prefix for cost. Ablating attention \emph{entirely} from that prefix
moves log-rank by only $-0.071$. Independently, permuting the gamma vectors' dimension order at
random scored $+0.039$ against the correct order's $+0.071$ --- a random permutation beating the
truth. The $12$-layer readout therefore has a noise band of $0.03$--$0.08$ nats and cannot
distinguish ``attention working'' from ``attention absent.'' Five sweeps had already been run on
it. At full depth the same ablation registers $-0.498$, an order of magnitude outside the band.

\begin{finding}
Before varying a component, ablate it. If removing the component entirely does not move the
metric by more than its noise band, the instrument cannot see that component and any negative
result about it is a statement about the instrument.
\end{finding}

We adopt two rules for the remainder: every sweep embeds a random-permutation null to establish
its own band, and every harness embeds an identity control that must reproduce a known value
exactly. Both caught errors --- the null in the gamma sweep, and an identity control that revealed
a mis-designed no-op arm in the basis experiment of \S\ref{sec:gap-basis}.

\subsection{Branch decomposition}

With a calibrated full-depth instrument, the gap decomposes cleanly. Ablations are scored on the
documents used for the floor; the ``both off'' arm reproduces $\ell_0$ to $0.0\times10^{0}$,
confirming the harness.

\begin{finding}
The reconstructed attention destroys $0.50$ nats. Removing it is the only configuration that
places the model below its $0$-layer floor. The feed-forward stack alone contributes $0.03$ nats,
against $7.7$ for healthy layers; both branches are effectively inert, and one is actively
harmful.
\end{finding}

Scaling the attention write by $s$ confirms the write carries no recoverable signal: $\ell$ is
monotone in $s$ above $s=0.05$, and the best scale improves on $s=0$ by $0.006$ nats, which we
regard as noise rather than a result. Removing the branch post-normalisation --- which inflates the
raw attention output by $\approx 44\times$ (\S\ref{sec:gap-amp}) --- lands at $7.0894$, statistically
identical to removing attention outright.

\subsection{Why the writes do not compound}\label{sec:gap-amp}

For a residual update $h \mapsto h + w$ we have
$\lVert h+w\rVert^2 = \lVert h\rVert^2 + 2\cos\theta\,\lVert h\rVert\lVert w\rVert + \lVert w\rVert^2$,
so growth is governed by the write's \emph{direction}. Measuring $\cos\theta$ and the relative
amplitude $\rho = \lVert w\rVert/\lVert h\rVert$ per layer:

The reconstruction's attention writes are essentially orthogonal to the residual, $60$--$80\times$
less aligned than healthy writes, and the predicted per-layer growth from its own
$(\cos\theta,\rho)$ is $1.021$ against $1.035$ observed --- the failure to compound is
\emph{fully} explained by direction, leaving no residual for another cause. Healthy feed-forward
writes are consistently \emph{negatively} aligned; ours are weakly positive.

Decomposing the attention write by stage isolates two amplitude faults. The raw attention output
is $0.0046$ of $\lVert h\rVert$ where healthy is $0.31$--$0.73$ --- roughly $100\times$ too small
\emph{before} any convention --- and the branch post-normalisation then inflates it $44\times$ to a
superficially normal $0.2044$. Conversely the raw feed-forward output is $0.6350$, squarely in the
healthy band, and the recovered out-gamma attenuates it $18\times$. The post-normalisation had been
masking the attention deficit in every amplitude check.

The sharpest single anomaly is positional. Measuring attention mass by relative distance:

Local and tail mass are normal; the entire positional deficit is the missing attention sink.
Four independent attempts to recover it all fail. (i) No choice of query/key normalisation
restores it (all arms $0.036$--$0.046$). (ii) Placing \emph{every one} of the $262{,}144$
vocabulary tokens at position $0$ yields a maximum sink of $0.047$, against an ordinary-token
reference band of $0.031\pm0.007$: it is not a wrong-\code{BOS} problem. (iii) Sharpening the
logits does not reveal a latent sink --- the ratio of sink mass to peak mass \emph{falls}
monotonically from $0.545$ to $0.051$ as temperature rises, so as attention approaches
$\arg\max$ it concentrates on positions other than $0$. (iv) Disabling RoPE entirely leaves the
sink at $0.057$, though a sink is a content mechanism that should survive without positional
encoding.

Position $0$ also differs \emph{qualitatively}, not by magnitude: $\cos(h_0, \bar h_{>0})$ is
$+0.150$ in the reconstruction and $-0.364$ / $-0.423$ in the controls, and the \code{BOS}
embedding row sits at the $9.7$th norm percentile where healthy \code{BOS} rows sit at the
$98.5$th and $85.6$th.

Two entries deserve comment. The \emph{kv-reuse} region --- $21$ layers that carry no keys or
values of their own and attend against layer $34$'s --- was the most speculative choice in the
entire teardown and is exonerated: removing all $21$ moves log-rank by $-0.073$, and the region is
not internally consistent (removing $L_{35\text{--}45}$ helps, removing $L_{46\text{--}55}$ hurts).
Conversely, removing the sparse mixture-of-experts block \emph{costs} $+1.20$ nats, so those layers
are load-bearing: they partially repair damage inflicted elsewhere, exactly as the feed-forward
branch partially repairs attention damage.

\subsection{What is missing}

The evidence admits a single consistent reading. No component is absent: all $218$ tensors are
present, decode with correct statistics, and are consumed by the forward pass. What is absent is
\emph{mutual consistency on the shared residual axis} --- tensors that write the $1536$-dimensional
residual and tensors that read it need not be in the same dimension order. Under such a mismatch
every tensor remains internally perfect while their composition produces noise, which is precisely
the measured signature: writes orthogonal to the residual, no attention sink for any token, and
damage distributed uniformly across all $56$ layers.

This also explains why so many tests returned clean: \emph{every static statistic we or prior work
employed is invariant to a permutation of that axis} --- row norms, singular spectra, palette code
frequencies, OV gain, QK participation. The tests were not wrong; they were structurally blind to
this class of error, in the same way the $12$-layer readout was blind to attention.

We can eliminate the enumerable part of the hypothesis. A layout mismatch produced by hardware
convention is a reshape-transpose: an axis written as $(a,b)$ and read as $(b,a)$. Since
$1536 = 2^9\cdot 3$ has $20$ divisors, the complete two-factor family is $18$ permutations, which
with three-factor arrangements around the accelerator's $16$-unit granularity gives $42$ distinct
candidates; applied to each of the two groups this is $84$ arms, and not one beats the identity
decode (best: $+0.472$ worse). Combined with the refutation of the accelerator's own stripe
permutation on validated plumbing, the structured portion of the basis hypothesis is closed. What
remains --- unstructured relabelings --- is neither brute-forceable nor motivated by any hardware
convention.

\subsection{Cross-model corroboration}

The same reconstruction methodology applied to \code{afmplus-v11.0-nano} --- a \emph{dense}
$3.33$\,B sibling with no mixture-of-experts, no routing metadata, and none of \code{ifp3}'s
architectural unknowns --- converges on identical behaviour: top-$1$ accuracy of exactly $0.00\%$
and an $\arg\max$ that reproduces the current token. Two independent decodes of two different
architectures, from different containers, do not share a per-model mistake. This is the strongest
evidence that the defect lies in the shared method or in what the assets contain, rather than in
any remaining ordering, constant, or scale in either model.

\subsection{Threats to validity}

We report the limits of the above candidly. The controls are two open-weight models of different
scale and family; where they disagree with each other by more than the subject differs from
either, we draw no conclusion. Several statistics reported here were initially over-read from
automated thresholds and subsequently corrected against their own tables --- notably a QK spectrum
normalised by ambient dimension rather than true rank, an attention-scale optimum of $0.006$ nats
reported as signal, and a no-op control that permuted weights without the gammas, embedding, and
tied readout. Each correction is retained in the record. Finally, the $12$-layer results predating
the ablation calibration constrain the instrument rather than the model, and are reported here
only where they were subsequently reproduced at full depth.

\subsection{Outlook}

The decisive instrument is not another static sweep. One layer of ground-truth intermediate
activations from Apple's own runtime would pin the residual basis directly and resolve in a single
comparison what $42$ controlled experiments could not. The execution route partly exists: the
shipped neural-engine program loads and runs from an unprivileged process through the public graph
API, and the model's own readout stage has been ground-truthed this way. The backbone stage,
however, is gated. A client request to run it is refused unless an \emph{inference request} for
the base-model network already exists, and creating one requires a set of Apple-signed-only
entitlements held by the system inference daemon.

We tested the one route to that ground truth that does not require creating the instance
ourselves: whether an inference request created by the entitled daemon --- driven legitimately
through the public high-level API --- is shared with a separate unprivileged client of the same
daemon. It is not. With a sanctioned session actively generating, our client's backbone request
still fails identically to the control (the accelerator performs zero inferences), and each
attempt is assigned its own distinct model instance. The request is scoped to the entitled client
connection, not deduplicated by asset identity, so it cannot be borrowed. This closes the last
route reachable without elevated privilege.

The boundary is therefore sharp and worth stating exactly, because it is not a boundary of the
recovered data. Every byte Apple ships is reversible and is reversed here; what is missing is a
single external reference against which to fix the residual-axis correspondence, and the platform
withholds that reference by policy rather than by any property of the assets. It becomes reachable
along one of three paths, none of which is a further analysis of the shipped files: an
Apple-granted research entitlement or debug build that surfaces per-node outputs; the same capture
performed on hardware whose owner has removed the entitlement enforcement; or an independent
un-pruned reference model to align the decoded weights against. Absent one of these we regard the
from-weights reconstruction as blocked, and report the boundary rather than a result.
