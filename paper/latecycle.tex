\section{A Decode Bug, and the Limits of Statistical Ordering}\label{sec:latecycle}

The preceding section reported six of seven weight roles positively confirmed and the remaining gap
as one tensor wide. Continued work overturned both claims. This section records a real decode defect
found by its cause rather than inferred from a statistic, establishes that the instrument used to
rank orderings has no discriminating power on the axis it was applied to, and reports that both
dynamic-observation routes are closed on measurement. The conclusion is a smaller claim than
\S\ref{sec:outtrans} made, and a better-supported one.

\subsection{The tile payload begins at \texorpdfstring{$+96$}{+96}, not \texorpdfstring{$+128$}{+128}}

Every per-input-channel magnitude profile in the recovered weights correlated across unrelated
tensors and layers at Pearson $0.907$ with Spearman $0.146$; a correctly-decoded reference model
gives $-0.03$ to $0.20$. High Pearson with near-zero rank correlation means a few extreme channels
shared by every decode---an artefact. They were input channels $1020$--$1023$, the last four, at
$3.2\times$ the median norm. Four input channels is exactly $64$ nibbles, or $32$ bytes.

The tile header is $96$ bytes, not $128$: a $16$-entry \textrm{fp16} codebook at $+0$ (cleanly
sorted), $32$ reserved zero bytes at $+32$, $16$ \textrm{fp16} scales at $+64$, and payload from
$+96$. The decoder had been treating $+96$--$+128$ as header, so every weight was displaced by four
input channels and the last four read past the payload.

\begin{finding}[the payload offset, proven structurally]\label{find:p96}
All $64$ examined tiles carry exactly zero in their final $32$ bytes: the layout is
$96 + 8192 + 32 = 8320 = \mathtt{0x2080}$, the $N$-class stride, with the trailing $32$ bytes being
$64$-byte alignment padding. The same $96$-byte header reproduces the other two tile classes exactly,
$96+4096\rightarrow\mathtt{0x1080}$ and $96+25600\rightarrow\mathtt{0x6480}$.
\end{finding}

\noindent The stride alone never disambiguated this, since $128 + \text{payload}$ also matches all
three classes---which is why it survived so long. Correcting it raises layer-$0$ attention sharpness
from $0.0468$ to $0.6058$ against a norm-matched random baseline of $0.0050$, and removes the outlier
artefact entirely ($3.20\rightarrow1.02$).

The finding was then confirmed on a \emph{second, independent build} of the same model, obtained by
mounting the shipped Cryptex image rather than reading the protected asset path: $700/775$ tiles
match the header layout and $680/775$ carry the zero tail, the remainder being the two other tile
classes which the test wrongly assumed $N$-class geometry for. Its weight map was re-derived
independently and reproduces the first build's tensors \emph{bit-exactly}.

\subsection{The architecture, from Apple's own manifest}

The \code{manifest.plist} inside the \code{mpsgraphpackage} names every module with tensor shapes
embedded in the key. Collapsing the layer index gives the seven roles directly:
\code{attention\_qkv\_transform} \code{lora\_0/1/2} at $1024\!\to\!1024$, $1024\!\to\!256$,
$1024\!\to\!256$; \code{attention\_output\_transform} at $1024\!\to\!1024$;
\code{feed\_forward\_hidden\_transform\_linear\_0/1} at $1024\!\to\!3200$; and
\code{feed\_forward\_output\_transform} at $3200\!\to\!1024$. This confirms the decomposition and
every shape from the shipped asset rather than by inference, and settles two hypotheses on the
record: $Q$, $K$ and $V$ are three \emph{separate} transforms, and gate and up are two \emph{separate}
linears---neither pair is fused or interleaved in storage.

\subsection{The ordering statistic has no power}

\S\ref{sec:outtrans} scored candidate orderings by a composition $z$-score; that instrument was
withdrawn, and replaced by teacher-forced negative log-likelihood against a hard reference
($\ln 262144 = 12.477$ nats) with random-permutation controls. The replacement produced a succession
of candidates that cleared control bands on multiple independent texts and then failed.

The decisive test is uniformity. A storage-order correction must hold for \emph{every} layer, since
the hardware lays all $24$ out identically.

\begin{finding}[the NLL oracle cannot decide the FFN axis]\label{find:nopower}
Applying the best surviving candidate permutation to one layer at a time improves $14$ of $24$
layers on one held-out text and $14$ of $24$ on another. A \emph{random} permutation applied the same
way improves $14$ of $24$. Per layer the candidate is indistinguishable from noise, and its
aggregate gain is $24$ scattered accidents composing favourably.
\end{finding}

\noindent The random row is the substantive result: perturbing a single layer's down-projection input
axis at random improves the model half the time. That is the signature of a tensor whose contribution
is already close to noise---no gradient of correctness exists, so all permutations score within one
band and their rankings are meaningless. This retroactively explains every ordering sweep in this
work, and the correct response is to stop running them rather than to run better-controlled ones.

\subsection{Both dynamic routes are closed}

\S\ref{sec:outtrans} proposed ground-truth activations as the remedy. They are not obtainable by the
methods available.

\emph{Core capture.} Four privileged captures produced five cores totalling ${\sim}40$\,GB. The first
three targeted the wrong process: \code{lsof} on the asset paths shows the $3$B and the $300$M held by
\emph{different instances} of the same service binary, and the $300$M host idles at $3.4$\,MB and
never becomes CPU-hot because it is the speculative-decoding draft model. Dumping the correct host
mid-generation, its resident size rose to $71$\,MB and its \code{program.odix} is present---but
scale-invariant cosine against the token embeddings actually used in that run peaks at $0.365$ over
both cores, indistinguishable from noise. Each captured generation emitted ${\sim}40$ tokens and
speculative decoding invokes the draft model for every token, so the model executed over a hundred
forward passes inside the window. The activations are not in user-process memory; they live in ANE
device memory that \code{save-core} cannot reach.

\emph{Logging.} The runtime logs \code{shape} and \code{strides} on every tensor load, redacted rather
than withheld. Unredaction now requires a signed logging profile: enabling
\code{private\_data:on} as root and forcing a model reload left $5{,}870$ lines redacted and zero
readable strides. Independently of that gate, these are graph \emph{boundary} shapes---inputs, KV
cache, logits---whereas the weight tile order is compiled into the \code{hwx} and never appears as a
runtime stride.

\subsection{Revised status}

\begin{observation}[what is and is not established]\label{obs:revised}
Established by structural proof or positive control: the tile format including the $+96$ payload
offset, cross-validated on two independent builds; the intra-bank slot map, which beats every
alternative chunk width by $7\times$; per-output scale indexing; the seven-role decomposition and all
shapes, from Apple's manifest; the token embedding and tokenizer; and that all recovered tensor
\emph{values} are trained matrices. Not established for any role: the element$\rightarrow$position
ordering.
\end{observation}

\noindent The claim in \S\ref{sec:outtrans} that six of seven roles were positively confirmed does not
survive; it rested on the composition oracle withdrawn there. The claim that the attention output
projection is stored transposed is weakened to an orientation asymmetry: the reference model is only
dimensionally valid one way while the recovered weights rise above floor the other way, but within an
orientation the metric is insensitive to scrambling, so it does not constitute recovered structure.
Under the same standard the recovered $V$ and $O$ move from ``broken'' to \emph{untested}---a totally
broken pair could not yield the sub-chance behaviour measured through layer $12$, where norm-matched
random weights never fall below chance at any depth.

The file format is solved. The arrangement is not, and this section establishes that it is reachable
neither by statistics on a non-functioning forward nor by observation of a runtime that keeps the
quantity of interest inside the accelerator.
