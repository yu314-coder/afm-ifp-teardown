\section{Recovered Model Specifications}\label{sec:modelspec}

This section consolidates every architectural quantity recovered for the three language backbones,
together with the physical layout of their weight containers. Values are marked \emph{stated} when
read directly from a shipped \code{metadata.\allowbreak json} or \code{config.\allowbreak json},
\emph{recovered} when derived from the binary and cross-checked, and \emph{provisional} where noted.

The remaining $38$ blocks lie \emph{after} every mapped layer (offsets \code{0xb327040}--\code{0xb889cc0};
layer $23$ ends at \code{0xb2c0800}) and compose as $32\,\code{N} + 2\,\code{s} + 4\,\code{L}$ --- exactly
one layer minus its $K$ and $V$ projections. Split by file order against a normal layer, gate, up and
down assemble to the exact expected shapes, leaving eight \code{N} blocks for $Q$ and $O$. This is a
complete, correctly-shaped \textbf{KV-reusing layer}, consistent with the KV-reuse the \code{odix} graph
already records elsewhere. Running it as a $25$th layer changes the reconstruction's output but does not
repair it, so it is reported as a structural result: the layer count should be treated as
\emph{provisional} pending a direct re-derivation from the \code{odix} op stream.

$n_{\mathrm{out}}$ output channels across \emph{all} inputs, and the header carries one \textrm{fp16}
scale per output channel.

\paragraph{Header layout} (all classes): \textrm{fp16}$[0{:}16]$ is the $16$-entry codebook,
\textrm{fp16}$[16{:}32]$ is zero padding, \textrm{fp16}$[32{:}48]$ (bytes $64{:}96$) is the per-output
scale vector --- the \code{s} class carrying exactly eight plausible values there, matching its eight
outputs per bank --- and the remaining $32$ bytes are unidentified.

\paragraph{Decode.} With the z-order of \S\ref{find:posread},
\[
  W[i,\;16\,b + o] \;=\; \mathrm{codebook}\!\left[\mathrm{nibble}\right] \cdot \mathrm{scale}[o],
  \qquad o = \mathrm{slot} \bmod 16, \quad i = \lfloor \mathrm{slot}/16 \rfloor,
\]
for bank $b$, validated by round-trip at correlation $0.981$. This is exact for the five roles the ANE
emits with $\mathrm{OutTrans}{=}0$ ($Q$, $K$, $V$, gate, up) and not yet resolved for the two emitted with
$\mathrm{OutTrans}{=}1$ ($O$, down); see \S\ref{find:outtrans}.

\paragraph{Embedding.} Recovered bit-exactly and validated semantically. The table is stored as an
$8$-way token interleave with a per-lane skew: for token $t$ and dimension vector $\mathbf{d}$,
\[
  \mathrm{nib}(t) \;=\; \mathrm{NIB}_0 + \lfloor t/8 \rfloor \cdot 8192 + 8\,\mathbf{d} + \ell + \mathrm{SKEW}[\ell],
  \qquad \ell = t \bmod 8,
\]
with $\mathrm{SKEW} = (0,0,0,0,8184,8184,8184,8184)$ and $8184 = 8\cdot1024 - 8$. Values are signed
\textrm{int4} with zero-point $0$, scaled by a $1024$-entry \textrm{fp16} per-dimension vector. On the
calibrated orthographic-pair oracle the recovered table scores
$\cos(\code{\_dog},\code{\_dogs}) = +0.648$ and $\cos(\code{\_king},\code{\_kings}) = +0.664$ against
cross-category controls at ${+}0.01$--${+}0.20$; the pooled margin over an id-matched control is
$+0.4565$. Embedding into the tied unembedding with no transformer layers ranks the correct
continuation token at $2213$ of $262{,}000$.

\paragraph{Expert storage.} The ANE program \code{binary\_0.hwx} ($441$\,MB) contains $83$ specialized
regions covering \code{compute\_logits} and \code{lora\_48\_ifp\{1,2,3\}\_r48} in extend/prompt variants,
dense-only and sparse-only, at sequence lengths $256$ through $8192$. Expert coefficients live in
runtime-patched mutable kernels --- \code{ifp1} in a $202$\,MB kernel, \code{ifp2} in $437$\,MB,
\code{ifp3} in $671$\,MB --- each patched from \code{ifp\_rasterized\_weights} via a content-hash
descriptor id. The gather is a runtime affine offset supplied by the driver, and the identical
machinery appears in dense-only regions, which establishes it as weight-blob relocation rather than
per-expert selection. No static router-index$\to$slot table exists in the \code{.hwx}.

\subsection{The dense backbone \code{afmplus-v11.0-nano}}

Stated in \code{metadata.\allowbreak json} (\code{model\_config: v11-nano}): context $4096$, hidden size
$2048$, FFN $6656$ gated SwiGLU, $56$ transformer layers emitted in two ANE segments ($35 + 21$),
grouped-query attention, RoPE, RMSNorm, adapter slots at LoRA ranks $16$ and $32$ (empty in the base),
prompt template \code{afm\_cider\_v2}. The \code{base.generic} image is $1.3$\,GB, i.e.\
${\approx}2$ bits per weight. The $3$B embedding asset of \S\ref{find:embshipped} declares
\code{embedding\_dim} $2048$ and therefore serves this backbone rather than the $1536$-wide sparse one.

\subsection{Weight codec, common to all three}

Four-bit LUT palettization: a $4$-bit index selects from a $16$-entry \textrm{fp16} codebook, scaled by
a per-block \textrm{fp16} factor, stored under an Apple Neural Engine tile permutation. For the $3$B the
de-swizzle is $W = v.\mathrm{reshape}(C_o/8,\,C_i/128,\,128,\,8).\mathrm{transpose}(0,3,1,2)$ with the
scale applied in raw index order \emph{before} de-swizzling. For the draft model the equivalent
transform is the bank z-order given above. Under a scale-decontaminated low-rank structure test,
genuine weights score $R \approx 9$ and noise $R \approx 1$; the decode scores $R = 13$--$69$.
